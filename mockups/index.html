Rethinking the home page

Less star chart, more show. Each direction keeps the diamond, the eight disciplines and the "connect every dot" idea — but trades the drifting constellation for structure, and puts event imagery on the page so festivals, concerts, conferences and galas all read at a glance. The two markets are interleaved rather than split down the middle: both are named, neither gets its own side of the fence.

About the images. There are no photos in the project yet, so every image slot renders as a duotone placeholder with its intended subject printed on it, cropped exactly where the real photo will sit. Dropping in real work is one line per slot — set --img:url('photos/mainstage.jpg') on that slot and the placeholder steps aside.
