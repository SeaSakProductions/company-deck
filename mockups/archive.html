05Two Tracks

A true 50/50 split with a drawn seam — Live on one side, Corporate on the other, each in its own vocabulary. The mark straddles the seam and is the only thing allowed to touch both worlds.

Below the fold it pays the idea off: a "same discipline, different room" table runs Advance, Load-In, Power, Labor, Show Call and Load-Out through both worlds side by side — and the Events/Clients rosters split into two columns.

02The Work Wall

The hero is the work: an asymmetric mosaic of stills — main stage next to gala, keynote next to crew call — interleaved so no corner of the wall belongs to one market.

Portfolio-first and the most familiar read for a production company. It lives or dies on the photography, so it needs six to eight genuinely good frames.

03The Through-Line

One horizontal spine — Advance, Load-In, Show, Load-Out — with the diamond anchoring show day. Each station's photo cross-fades through a different venue: amphitheater, ballroom, expo hall, fairground.

The closest fit to "connecting all the dots from every aspect of planning": it shows the process, and proves the range by running the same line through both kinds of event.

04Systems Board

The eight disciplines laid out like a production plot or patch panel — orthogonal, deliberate traces that draw in and settle, over a photo band that slowly cycles event types.

Leans into the "Solutions" half of the name: infrastructure and control. Reads the most corporate-credible while still looking like show world, and needs the fewest photos.

About the images. There are no photos in the project yet, so every image slot renders as a duotone placeholder with its intended subject printed on it, cropped exactly where the real photo will sit. Dropping in real work is one line per slot — set --img:url('photos/mainstage.jpg') on that slot and the placeholder steps aside.
